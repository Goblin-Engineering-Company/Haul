# Haul 2026.11.19.1

**Haul 2026.11.19.1: Skill-Up Fix**

A one-fix release.

**No more skill-up error.** Sometimes the "Your skill in ... has increased" message arrives in a protected form that addons aren't allowed to read. Haul tried to read it anyway and threw an error every time that message came in. Haul now leaves the sealed envelope alone and gets on with counting your loot. Your skill level still updates the next time you open the profession window.

Your sessions, history and settings are untouched. The ledgers are exactly where you left them.
