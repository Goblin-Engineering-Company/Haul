# 💰 Haul

**If you farm it, Haul has the numbers.**

Haul is a farming session tracker that captures *everything*: every loot drop, every coin, experience, reputation, currencies, kills, and gathering. It values the lot and rolls it into a clean movable bar that tells you what this farm actually earns per hour.

## The Custom Header Bar

A compact, movable readout you design yourself with template fields. Session time, haul value, gross, gold per hour, cash, arranged however you like. Glance at it mid-pull, or leave it running all night.

## Sessions that know when to stop counting

- **Pause actually pauses.** Anything you pick up while paused (a mailbox run, a vendor detour) is captured in the log but stays out of the session's totals and gold per hour. Your farm rate stays honest.
- **Instances, set aside and picked back up.** Turn on "New session on instances" and zoning into a dungeon parks your open-world session untouched while the instance tracks on its own. Leave, and the old one picks up exactly where it was.
- **Keep, merge, combine.** Every run banks to your history when you start a new one. Fold tonight's run into last night's and build a real record of what each farm is worth.

## Real values, your price source

Item values come from vendor prices or your auction data, with TSM and Auctionator supported. Per item, visible, priced. Exclude anything you don't count as income with a click. Gray quality and bound items value at vendor automatically. Mail gold is tracked as its own category, and returned auction items are never counted as new income, so a listing that came back doesn't read as profit.

## Beyond loot

- **Experience.** XP per session and per hour, attributed to kills, quests, gathering, and discovery.
- **Reputation and currencies.** Every faction tick and currency gain, per session.
- **Kills and gathering.** What you killed, what it dropped, which nodes you hit.
- **The Data page.** Browse every saved session in detail: items, sources, timings, the works.

## Optional: your farm history, everywhere

Pair with **GEC Uplink**, our free desktop companion, and your sessions sync to your profile at [goblineng.co](https://goblineng.co). Gold totals, best sessions, and per-hour rates, browsable outside the game. Entirely optional. Haul is fully functional standalone.
